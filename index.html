About

Clariss Crisol is a no-code and low-code automation specialist focused on helping businesses run smarter through connected systems. With experience spanning GoHighLevel, Zapier, n8n, Asana, ClickUp, Pipedrive, and Slack, she designs end-to-end workflows that eliminate repetitive tasks, improve team coordination, and accelerate operations.

Her project work includes building AI-driven sales pipeline automations for Selr.ai and creating member onboarding and engagement systems for FiTribe — integrating payment events, CRM data, and real-time notifications into unified, reliable workflows.

She brings both the technical fluency to work directly with REST APIs and webhooks, and the systems thinking to translate complex business processes into clean, maintainable automations. Available for freelance and contract engagements — fully remote.
